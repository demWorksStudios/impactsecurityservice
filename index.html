Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding guard deployment, compliance, training, and supervision.

How quickly can you deploy security guards at our site?

We can deploy an initial security squad within 24 to 48 hours following a site security survey and signed agreement. For short-term events or emergency bouncer requirements, rapid same-day deployment is also available.

Are your security guards police verified and background checked?

Yes, 100%. Every guard undergoes identity verification, residential address validation, reference checks, and local police verification records before being posted to any commercial or residential facility.

What happens if a security guard is absent or falls ill?

We maintain a dedicated 15% reserve roster of trained personnel. Our field supervisor immediately dispatches a replacement guard within 2 hours to ensure your post is never left vacant.

Do you manage full statutory compliance (PF, ESIC, GST)?

Yes. Impact Security Service is fully GST registered (GSTIN: 19DUMPA9757H1ZO) and compliant with all statutory labor laws including Provident Fund (PF), ESIC medical coverage, and Minimum Wages regulations.

Which geographic areas do you cover?

Our primary operational footprint spans Howrah (Liluah, Belur, Bally, Kona Expressway, Dhulagarh, Sankrail) and Greater Kolkata (Salt Lake, New Town, Park Street, Alipore, Behala, BT Road) and adjoining industrial belts across West Bengal.
